The year — a sequential heatmap

Less More

One hue, light→dark, neutral for empty — the heatmap rule. Every adjacent step is separated by ≥1.25× in luminance, so the ramp stays readable in greyscale and to a fully colour-blind viewer. Each cell also carries a text label, so colour is never the only channel. Gaps are neutral, never red — the grid shows what you did, never what you didn't.

Palette

One accent, used for at most three things: the recording state, the current day, the active selection. A single-accent palette has no colour pairs to confuse, which makes it inherently colour-blind safe — the meaning lives in lightness and position.

17/26 body is the single most important number here. The 26px line-height is what makes long text feel readable rather than cramped. Never let it be tightened.

Spacing — 8pt grid

24pt screen margins, not 16. Sixteen is the framework default, and defaults look like defaults.

Principles

Do

  • Content sits on the page — no cards, no shadows
  • One accent, three uses, nothing more
  • 200ms motion, only to explain space
  • 48×48dp minimum touch targets
  • Every colour state also has a shape or label
  • Dark by default; light is chosen, not inverted
  • Warm near-black and off-white, never #000 or #FFF

Don't

  • Streaks, badges, guilt — gaps are never red
  • Gradients, glass, glow, confetti
  • Content previews on the lock screen
  • Colour as the only carrier of meaning
  • A formatting toolbar between you and your feelings
  • Illustrations, mascots, onboarding carousels
  • Any hex code written inside a widget
